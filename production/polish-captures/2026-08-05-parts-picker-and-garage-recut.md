## Proposed change

Build the post-combat part reward picker as a near-clone of the shipped
`CardRewardPickerController`: a hidden fullscreen UI Toolkit surface that chains
off the card picker's `OnPickResolved`, reads `RunState.PendingPartOffer`,
renders up to `FlatPartRewardSource.OfferSize` (3) choices, and resolves through
the already-built `RunSession.AcceptPartChoice` / `SkipPartChoice`. `CombatHud`'s
`NotifyRewardClaimed` subscription moves from the card picker to the part picker
so the beacon advances exactly once at the end of the chain.

This is slice 1 of a re-cut of Phase 2.5's UI work, triggered by a 2026-08-05
design mockup that reframed the "fullscreen inventory screen" as a **modal
inside the already-shipped Chopshop screen**.

## Final-game picture this serves

The parts axis is the last unbuilt pillar of the 1.0 loop: combat drops parts →
parts change the vehicle → the vehicle changes which cards are worth playing.
Slices 2.5C A1–A3 landed the entire model layer (RunInventory, PartOffer,
persistence, the swap-only Vehicle verb) but shipped it **dark by data** — the
biome pool is empty, so nothing drops.

The re-cut turns that axis on in the cheapest correct order. Three findings
shrank the work dramatically:

1. The garage is a **modal in the existing Chopshop**, not a new screen.
   `ChopshopScreen.uxml` already reserves `#modal-container` for exactly this,
   and `choice-4` ("Browse parts. (soon)") is already on screen, disabled.
2. The mockup's centrepiece — vehicle plus per-slot callout pins — **already
   renders**. `ChopshopWorkbenchController` poses the real `PlayerVehicle`
   prefab in world space via `VehicleRestPose` and `VehicleBarStack.BindForWorkbench`
   already builds a `SlotTargetRing` at each hand-placed `VehicleHudAnchors`
   entry.
3. The mockup's "VENDOR" panel is the already-shipped `#dialogue-panel`.

User decisions taken 2026-08-05 that shape the whole re-cut:

- **Equip is Chopshop-ONLY.** Supersedes the previously-locked "install locked
  in Combat, open on RunMap" rule. This cancels the RunMap garage host — the
  single most expensive item in the plan, being the only one needing a
  `VehicleVisual` + pose + anchor catalog stood up outside a beacon root — and
  makes the fullscreen icon router moot for inventory.
- **Bodywork is a parallel authoring track.** Panels are layout-driven, so the
  Bodywork group is absent (not empty, not stubbed) until slots are authored.
- **Dodge is deferred; `StatKind`/`StatModifier` types are kept.** The false doc
  claim in `StatKind.cs` gets fixed in slice 2.

## Sequencing refinement (deviation from the TD verdict, deliberate)

The TD's slice 1 filled `PartRewardPool_Biome1.asset` and deleted
`EveryPartRewardPool_IsEmpty_UntilThePickerUiShips` atomically with the picker.
**This capture does neither**, on purpose.

A part that drops before the garage exists lands in `RunInventory` and can never
be equipped — a reward with no payoff, that opens the save-bricking window five
slices before any player value arrives. Keeping the gate test in place longer is
free and strictly safer. The pool fill plus the gate-test deletion move to the
**final** slice, after the equip verb works, where they remain atomic with each
other.

This does not weaken the TD's constraint; it strengthens it. The rule was never
"delete the gate early", it was "never let the pool be non-empty while no
resolver can clear a latched offer."

## BLOCKER — prefab drift must be baked first

`production/session-state/prefab-drift-pending.json` currently lists **Combat,
PlayerVehicle, MainBar, HudAnchors** as carrying designer-side Prefab-Mode edits
that have not been baked back into `CombatPrefabAuthor.cs`.

Mounting the picker requires editing `CombatPrefabAuthor.cs` and re-running
"Author Combat Prefab", which would **silently discard every unbaked edit on
those four prefabs**. Per `feedback_pre_author_capture_protocol` and
`feedback_bake_designer_edits`, the drift must be read out of the prefab YAML,
diffed against what the author script generates, baked into source as consts /
seeds / per-archetype entries, and captured in its own
`2026-08-05-prefab-drift-*.md` file BEFORE any re-author is proposed.

**This slice cannot complete until that bake lands.** The pure-C#/UXML portion
(controller, element, UXML, USS, tests) is unblocked and can proceed first; only
the author-script mount and the prefab regeneration are gated.

## Technical Director Review

**Verdict:** RESHAPE (both passes) — reshaped and adopted; see deviation above
**Spawned at:** 2026-08-05 (two consecutive TD passes)
**Agent transcript:** condensed to the binding rulings; full reasoning captured
in the session transcript.

### Pass 1 — slice shaping (pre-mockup)

- **Q1, equip verb:** `RunSession.EquipFromInventory(string slotId, int instanceId)
  → PartInstance`, body on `RunController.CommitEquipFromInventory`. **Atomicity
  is achieved by ordering, not try/catch**: `TryGet` → `SwapPart` → `Remove` →
  `Add`. `Vehicle.SwapPart` runs all four throw conditions (null args, unknown
  slotId, `!slot.HasPart`, `RequireInstallable`) strictly before its first
  mutating statement, so a throw leaves both objects untouched. Removing the row
  first is the bug: a MountDirection mismatch would delete the part permanently.
  Add an explicit non-null guard on the outgoing part between `SwapPart` and
  `Add` — unreachable today on the player vehicle, but it converts a future
  silent part-dissolution into a loud bug.
- **Q2, router:** ship no router. One button, one handler, no type. A coordinator
  built for one entry when two of its three eventual entries are still
  placeholder-shaped is scaffolding the next slice reshapes. *(Superseded — the
  Chopshop-only decision made the router moot entirely.)*
- **Q3, `VehicleStateDto` → `run.session_core`:** CONFIRMED, and worse than
  stated. `RunSceneHost.Initialize` gates on five DTOs and `LoadedVehicleState`
  is not among them, while `RunInventoryDto` **is** a member. A vehicle-state
  skip therefore deletes the equipped dropped part **and duplicates the authored
  part it replaced** — sellable at the Chopshop for real scrap. No SchemaVersion
  bump (membership is a host-side gate, invisible to the wire; bumping would wipe
  saves for a change the wire cannot see). No migration. New blast radius: a
  corrupt `run.vehicle_state` now regenerates the whole run. Accepted — a
  wrong-but-continuing run is worse, because the player cannot see the
  divergence.
- **Q4, `PartRarity`:** deferrable with no bridge. Absence is not a stub. Rarity
  needs drop-rate weights, which means `FlatPartRewardSource` stops being flat —
  its own tuning slice. Ship rows with **no** rarity affordance, not a grey
  placeholder tint.
- **Q5, ordering:** the save-group change must precede the equip verb, never
  trail it.

### Pass 2 — garage composition (post-mockup)

- **Q1, screen identity:** ONE garage element, bound through a `readonly struct
  GarageContext` carrying two **independent nullable capability collaborators**
  (equip, trade). Null = axis off. This is ADR-0013 sibling composition, not an
  ADR-0011 bimodal mode flag: the read surface is byte-identical in every host,
  only the verbs differ. Explicitly **declined** the `BindForCombat` /
  `BindForWorkbench` sibling-verb precedent — `VehicleBarStack.cs:382` pays for
  it with a per-frame refresh forked into two parallel bodies, which is drift to
  avoid copying, not a pattern to follow.
- **Q2, stacking:** the view MAY stack, under three invariants. **I1** — group
  key derived from every `IPartData` member, never `PartId` alone (an upgraded
  and a stock Hunter MG share a PartId and must never merge; `choice-3` "Upgrade
  a weapon" is already on screen). **I2** — no verb operates on a group; all take
  `instanceId`, resolving lowest-first so resume can never change which physical
  row a click consumes. **I3** — an EditMode test asserts field-equality across a
  group, so the day a per-instance roll lands the test goes red and forces the
  un-stack before the UI ships a lie.
- **Q3, stats:** `DMG` on a part **rejected permanently** — damage lives on
  cards (`WeaponAttackEffect.Damage`); the part contributes only the launch gate,
  so a DMG readout would teach the player something false about the combat
  system. `DODGE` deferred: `StatKind.DodgeRate` exists but the carrier field,
  the aggregator, and — decisively — **any consumer** are all missing;
  `DamagePipeline` performs no dodge roll, so the number would move on swap and
  be mechanically inert. Render `Vehicle.MaxArmor` + `StructuralHp` at vehicle
  level and `MaxHp` + `ArmorContribution` per part — all real, all move on swap.
- **Q4, vehicle render:** in scope at the Chopshop at near-zero cost, already on
  screen. **Load-bearing constraint:** `ChopshopScreen.uxml` sets `#root` to
  `picking-mode="Ignore"` so world-space clicks reach the hit zones; flipping it
  to `Position` **silently kills repair-mode hover**. The garage must mount as a
  `#modal-container` child and leave the vehicle region uncovered. Bodywork panel
  is layout-driven — the group is absent, not empty.
- **Q5, drag:** out of scope for v1. Select-then-place is canonical *and better*:
  selecting a part reveals fit before any gesture, so compatible slots highlight
  and incompatible ones dim — strictly better than drag-to-discover. A garage
  drag would also be the project's first cross-stack drag (UI Toolkit card →
  world-space UGUI hit zone across a picking-Ignore root). The vendor hint
  "Drag parts onto the vehicle to check fit" is a **stopgap visual** and must be
  rewritten. Drag layers on later over the identical verb, zero model change.
- **Q6, re-cut:** picker first — it needs no garage, no equip verb, and no
  persistence change, because `AcceptPartChoice` only writes `RunInventory`,
  which is already a session_core member.

### Live drift the TD found (not caused by this slice)

- `StatKind` / `StatModifier`: **zero references project-wide**, and
  `StatKind.cs` documents a `PartDefinitionSO._statModifiers` field that does not
  exist. ADR-0011 forbidden-pattern #4 (vestigial enum) plus an actively
  misleading comment. Kept per user decision; doc fixed in slice 2.
- `VehicleBodyworkAnchors`: prescribed by ADR-0017 §207-209, absent.
- **PartId divergence:** `Part_Scout_MachineGun.asset` carries
  `_partId: scout_machine_gun`; the headless fallback in `RunSceneHost.cs:1331`
  installs `scout_machinegun`. Harmless today because nothing keys off PartId —
  becomes a silent icon-and-tooltip failure the moment slice 2's catalog lands.
  Fixed in slice 2, before anything reads it.
